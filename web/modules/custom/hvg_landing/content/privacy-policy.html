1. Who I am

Paddy de Burca ("I", "me") is the data controller for personal information processed through hivelog.eu and the Vinculum app, the beekeeping logbook HiveLog and its companion app.

  • Name: Paddy de Burca
  • Email: info@hivelog.eu

2. What information I collect

I only collect information you give me directly, or that your use of the service produces:

  • Account details — username, email address and password when you register. The password is stored only as a one-way hash, so I cannot read it. A demo account has a username and password only: no email address is collected.
  • Profile details you choose to add — a picture, your time zone, and your Central Beehive Registration (CBR) number.
  • Your beekeeping records — apiaries (names and locations), hives, queens, inspections, observations, seasonal calendar entries, inventory, purchases, products and yields, the photos and notes you attach. They are visible to you and to the people you add as members of an apiary, and to nobody else.
  • Two-factor authentication — if you turn it on, the secret used to generate your codes, stored encrypted.
  • The Vinculum app — signs in through your account. The app keeps its sign-in tokens on your phone; signing out revokes them.
  • Messages — anything you send me through a form on this site or by email.
  • Technical data — standard web server and security logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) and the short-lived records that limit repeated sign-in attempts.

I do not collect sensitive personal information (such as health, ethnicity or beliefs), and I do not buy or receive information about you from third parties. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of what you do on the site.

3. Why I process it, and on what legal basis

  • To provide what you asked for (contract): running your account and keeping your records.
  • To run the site safely (legitimate interests): sign-in limits, the anti-robot check, two-factor authentication, security logs and fixing problems.
  • With your consent, for anything optional, which you can withdraw at any time.

I do not use your information for marketing, profiling or automated decision-making.

4. Who I share it with

I do not sell your information and I do not share it with advertising networks. It is shared only with:

  • Service providers who host the website and deliver its email (OVHcloud, France), acting under my instructions.
  • An anti-robot check (Friendly Captcha) on sign-up and forms, which verifies a small puzzle solved in your browser. It does not use cookies and is not used to identify or profile you.
  • Authorities where the law requires it.

Your information stays within the EU/EEA.

5. Cookies

The site uses only the cookies it needs to work: your login session and your cookie-consent choice. There are no analytics or advertising cookies. You can review or change your choice at any time with "My privacy settings" in the site footer.

6. How long I keep it

  • Demo accounts: the account and everything entered in it are deleted automatically 7 days after it was created.
  • Your account and records: for as long as you have an account. When you close your account, your personal details are deleted, and the records in your apiaries are deleted or anonymised on request.
  • Messages: deleted 12 months after you send them.
  • Server and security logs: kept for a limited time, no longer than 12 months.

7. How I protect it

The site is served over HTTPS, passwords are stored as hashes, two-factor authentication is available for every account, and access to your records is limited to you and the members you add. No internet service can be guaranteed 100% secure, but I take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the Danish Data Protection Act you can ask me to:

  • show you the information I hold about you (access);
  • correct it (rectification);
  • delete it (erasure), where no legal retention duty applies;
  • restrict or object to how I use it;
  • hand it over in a portable format (portability); and
  • withdraw any consent, at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

You can review and update most of your details yourself in your account. For anything else, email info@hivelog.eu and I will respond in accordance with applicable law.

If you believe I am processing your information unlawfully, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet, www.datatilsynet.dk) or to the data protection authority in your own EEA country.

9. Changes to this notice

If I change this notice, I will update the date at the top and, for material changes, post a notice on the site or contact you directly.
