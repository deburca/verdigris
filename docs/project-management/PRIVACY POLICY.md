## 1. Who we are

Stutteri Hestehøj ("we", "us") is the data controller for personal
information processed through our websites — stutteri-hestehoj.dk and
hestehoj.dk — and our services: the sale of Icelandic horses and the
hourly rental of our training facilities (Oval Track, Manège, Lunge
Ring).

## 2. What information we collect

We only collect information you give us directly:

- **Account details** — username, email address, and password, when
  you register or we create an account for you.
- **Rider membership** — your account status and the date your
  account was approved. The liability contract and emergency contact
  details are held on a paper contract managed by our staff, not on
  this website.
- **Bookings and orders** — the facilities and times you book, horses
  you purchase or place a deposit on, credit packs you buy, and your
  billing name and address.
- **Payment details** — we accept payment by bank transfer and cash
  only. We see the details of your transfer on our bank statement; we
  never collect or store card numbers.
- **Messages** — anything you send us through the contact form or by
  email.
- **Technical data** — standard web server logs (IP address, pages
  visited) used for security and troubleshooting.

We do not collect sensitive personal information (such as health,
ethnicity, or beliefs) and we do not buy or receive information about
you from third parties. Riders must be 18 or over to sign a rider
contract independently. Riders under 18 may participate if a responsible
adult (parent or legal guardian) signs the contract on their behalf;
we do not collect or store the responsible adult's details on this
website — those are held on the paper contract.

## 3. Why we process it, and on what legal basis

- **To provide what you asked for** (contract): managing your account,
  memberships, bookings, orders, deposits, and refunds.
- **Because the law requires it** (legal obligation): keeping
  accounting records of orders and payments under the Danish
  Bookkeeping Act.
- **To run the site safely** (legitimate interests): security logs,
  fraud prevention, and fixing problems.
- **In an emergency** (vital interests): contacting your emergency
  contact if something happens to you at our facilities.
- **With your consent**, for anything optional — which you can
  withdraw at any time.

We do not use your information for marketing, profiling, or automated
decision-making.

## 4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with
advertising networks. It is shared only with:

- **Service providers** who host our website (OVHcloud, France) and
  deliver our email (Open-Xchange GmbH, Germany), acting under our
  instructions.
- **Authorities and advisers** where the law requires it — for
  example, our accountant and the tax authorities.
- **A buyer of the business**, if Stutteri Hestehøj is ever sold or
  merged, in which case this notice continues to apply.

Both providers are based within the EU. Your information does not
leave the EU/EEA.

## 5. Cookies

The site uses only the cookies it needs to work: your login session
and your cookie-consent choice. We use no analytics and no advertising
or tracking cookies. You can review or change your choices at any time
via "My privacy settings" in the site footer.

## 6. How long we keep it

- **Orders, invoices, and payment records**: 5 years from the end of
  the financial year, as required by the Danish Bookkeeping Act.
- **Your account, bookings, and membership records**: for as long as
  you have an account with us. When you close your account, your
  personal records are deleted immediately. Operational records that
  reference your account (such as booking history) are anonymised and
  no longer linked to you personally.
- **Contact messages**: deleted 12 months after you send them.

Where a record must be kept for legal reasons after you close your
account (such as an invoice), we keep only that record, no longer used
for anything else, and delete it when the retention period ends.

## 7. How we protect it

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect
your information. No internet service can be guaranteed 100% secure,
but access to your information is limited to those who need it.

## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the Danish Data Protection Act you can ask us to:

- show you the information we hold about you (access);
- correct it (rectification);
- delete it (erasure), where no legal retention duty applies;
- restrict or object to how we use it;
- hand it over in a portable format (portability); and
- withdraw any consent, at any time, without affecting earlier
  processing.

If you have an account, you can review and update most of your details
yourself at stutteri-hestehoj.dk/user. For anything else — including
if you bought a horse without creating an account — email
info@stutteri-hestehoj.dk and we will respond in accordance with
applicable law.

If you believe we are processing your information unlawfully, you can
complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet,
www.datatilsynet.dk) or to the data protection authority in your own
EEA country.

## 9. Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top and, for
material changes, post a notice on the site or contact you directly.
